The app recolors the upholstery and fits both models to scene dimensions at runtime. It omits the chair's material-example label. The files bundled here are the upstream GLBs.
The photograph in `studio-garden.jpg` is a Studio Garden backplate by Sergej Majboroda. [Source and CC0 license](https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_garden).

## Photo detection

The local detector uses Google's unmodified SSD Lite MobileNet V2 COCO model weights and TensorFlow.js. [Model and Apache 2.0 license](https://www.kaggle.com/models/tensorflow/ssdlite-mobilenet-v2/tfJs/default/1) · [COCO-SSD wrapper source](https://github.com/tensorflow/tfjs-models/tree/master/coco-ssd)

The sample capture (`sample-capture.jpg`) is *Living Room with a Grey Couch* by Lisa Anna. [Photo source](https://www.pexels.com/photo/living-room-with-a-grey-couch-19846371/) · [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/)
